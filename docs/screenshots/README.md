# LeadPilot AI product screenshots

Save clean application-only product captures in this directory:

- `dashboard.png` — dashboard KPIs, pipeline overview, and Discovery Intelligence
- `companies.png` — company directory, filters, and lead status rows
- `discovery.png` — Discovery metrics, filters, scan history, and actions
- `discovery-report.png` — score cards, health checks, findings, and opportunities

Use a 1440×900 viewport where practical. Do not include browser tabs, personal
notifications, unrelated applications, or sensitive company data.
